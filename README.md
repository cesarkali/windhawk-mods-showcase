## 🏝️ Mac Island & Dock

An island at the top of the screen like the macOS menu bar and the iPhone's Dynamic Island (clock, control center, notifications, media, tray icons), and a macOS-like dock instead of the taskbar.

## 🎙️ AI Voice Typing

Press a shortcut, speak, and your words are typed where the cursor is, in any app: transcribed and cleaned up by AI in your style (natural, professional, prompt) or translated. Works with a free key from Groq, Gemini, Mistral and others.

Want a full app instead of a mod, with live preview, offline mode and per-app styles? See **[FlowVoice](https://github.com/cesarkali/Flow-Voice)**.

**[Install on Windhawk →](https://windhawk.net/mods/ai-voice-typing)** · [Source code](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/main/mods/ai-voice-typing.wh.cpp)

## 🖥️ Windows Terminal Start Minimized

When Windows Terminal opens at sign-in, it starts minimized or straight in the notification area. Requires Terminal's "Launch on machine startup" option.

**[Install on Windhawk →](https://windhawk.net/mods/windows-terminal-start-minimized)** · [Source code](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/blob/main/mods/windows-terminal-start-minimized.wh.cpp)

## How to install

1. Install [Windhawk](https://windhawk.net) (free).
2. Open the mod's link above and click **Install**, or search its name in Windhawk's **Explore** tab.

## Bugs and ideas

Issues are turned off here on purpose. Please open an issue in [ramensoftware/windhawk-mods](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/issues) with the mod's name in the title, so everything stays in one place.

## 🇧🇷 Em português

Mods gratuitos e de código aberto que mudam o Windows 11, publicados no catálogo oficial do [Windhawk](https://windhawk.net):

- **[Ilha e Dock estilo Mac](https://windhawk.net/mods/mac-island-dock)**: uma ilha no topo da tela como a barra de menus do macOS e a Dynamic Island do iPhone, e uma dock como a do macOS.
- **[Alt+Tab Flip 3D (estilo Vista)](https://windhawk.net/mods/alt-tab-flip-3d)**: troca o Alt+Tab por uma pilha 3D de janelas ao vivo, como no Windows Vista.
- **[Digitação por Voz com IA](https://windhawk.net/mods/ai-voice-typing)**: aperte um atalho, fale, e o texto é digitado onde está o cursor, em qualquer app, limpo pela IA no seu estilo ou traduzido, com uma chave grátis (Groq, Gemini, Mistral e outros). Quer um app completo? Conheça o [FlowVoice](https://github.com/cesarkali/Flow-Voice).
- **[Windows Terminal iniciar minimizado](https://windhawk.net/mods/windows-terminal-start-minimized)**: o Terminal já abre minimizado ou direto na área de notificação no logon.

**Como instalar:** instale o [Windhawk](https://windhawk.net) (grátis), abra o link do mod e clique em **Install**, ou procure o nome dele na aba **Explore**.

**Bugs e ideias:** abra uma issue em [ramensoftware/windhawk-mods](https://github.com/ramensoftware/windhawk-mods/issues) com o nome do mod no título (pode ser em português).
